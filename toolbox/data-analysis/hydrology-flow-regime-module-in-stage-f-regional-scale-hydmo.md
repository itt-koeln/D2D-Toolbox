# Hydrology – Flow regime module in stage F (regional scale) (HYDMOD-F)

*Posted 2018-06-21*

**Prepared by: Anja Fischenich**

---

**1.Background**

A natural hydrological regime is essential for biodiversity conservation and the sustainability, structure, and functioning of aquatic ecosystems. Increasing alteration of natural flow regimes due to human activities places aquatic ecosystems under increasing risk and can lead to environmental degradation.

To be able to assign a high ecological status for water bodies, the WFD explicitly requires the assessment of hydromorphological elements.

---

**2. Principle**

HYDMOD-F
- Swiss method describing the hydrological conditions of a region by recording anthropogenic interventions and assessing their impacts on the natural nature of the flow regime or the degree of naturalness.

- Intervention-driven approach.
**Method:**

Step 1: Identification of interventions that impact on the flow regime and creation of an inventory

Step 2: Evaluation of interventions

Step 3: Designation of significantly influenced river sections

Step 4: Assessment of river sections using nine evaluation parameters and time series data

HYDMOD-FIT: electronic too for IT-based application, standardization and reduction of application effort

For more info see:
<http://www.modul-stufen-konzept.ch/fg/module/hydro/hm/index_EN>
<http://www.modul-stufen-konzept.ch/fg/module/hydro/detail/index>

---

**3. Manual**
- Input data: time series of daily hydrological data of at least 5 to 8 years (depending on the parameter)

- Restriction: Only for a regional overview.

- Weaknesses: Huge effort for data collection and data processing needed, not all interventions are considered

---
