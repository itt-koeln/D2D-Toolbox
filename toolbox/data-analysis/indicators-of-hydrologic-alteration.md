# Indicators of hydrologic alteration

*Posted 2018-06-21*

**Prepared by: Anja Fischenich**

---

**1.Background**

A natural hydrological regime is essential for biodiversity conservation and the sustainability, structure, and functioning of aquatic ecosystems. Increasing alteration of natural flow regimes due to human activities places aquatic ecosystems under increasing risk and can lead to environmental degradation.

To be able to assign a high ecological status for water bodies, the WFD explicitly requires the assessment of hydromorphological elements.

---

**2. Principle**

Software that calculates the characteristics of the natural and altered hydrological regimes and summarises large periods of hydrological data into ecologic relevant hydrologic parameters.

The program calculates a total of 67 parameters of hydrologic alterations and environmental flow components and enables to compare pre-impact and post-impact periods as well as conducting a trend analysis for a single time period.

This method uses a deductive approach since the software uses time series for its calculations. Therefore, it contains a high risk of uncertainty, especially when assessing hydrologic alterations in complex watersheds.

---

**3. Manual**
- Input data: Complete time series of daily hydrological data of at least 20 years (35 or more years recommended). In case of missing data within the time series, the software automatically runs a linear interpolation over these gaps.

- How to install it: No need to install; just download the software under <https://www.conservationgateway.org/ConservationPractices/Freshwater/EnvironmentalFlows/MethodsandTools/IndicatorsofHydrologicAlteration/Pages/IHA-Software-Download.aspx>
The folder includes the software as well as example data.

- The IHA V 7.1 Tutorial which you can download at
<https://www.conservationgateway.org/Files/Pages/iha-v-71-tutorial.aspx> explains some basic features of the software and allows you to set up a basic analysis. In addition, it will take you through some more advanced features of the software.

- For information about the application of the program, please see the User’s Manual:
<https://www.conservationgateway.org/ConservationPractices/Freshwater/EnvironmentalFlows/MethodsandTools/IndicatorsofHydrologicAlteration/Documents/IHAV7.pdf>
