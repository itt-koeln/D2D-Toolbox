## Background

Working with a time series, especially raster products, can be very time consuming and challenging without the use of programming. This module teaches the use of R to download, analyse and evaluate remote sensing and reanalysis products in an efficient and robust manner.

## Principle

The module was adapted from a training workshop given to specialists from the Nile Basin Initiative. The focus of the content is the evaluation of the accuracy and reliability of remote sensing products by comparisons with ground-based measurements. The content of the modules focuses primarily on evaluation of remote sensing-based precipitation and actual evapotranspiration products, as well as closing the water balance using discharge measurements over selected catchments.

## Manual

The module consists of seven presentations:

1. Introduction to programming in R (Part 1)
2. Introduction to programming in R (Part 2)
3. Downloading and Importing Data
4. Rasters and Shapefiles
5. Visualising Products
6. Water Balance Evaluation
7. Point to Pixel Evaluation.
