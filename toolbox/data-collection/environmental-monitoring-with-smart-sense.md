# Environmental Monitoring with Smart Sense

*Posted 2023-02-28*

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2023_02_Weather-station-Gymnich-latest-530x707.jpeg)
***Weather station***

**Prepared by: Henny Grotehusmann**

**Background**

The ITT Smart Sense Weather Station is continuously measuring weather parameters of the surrounding area. Via the mobile network, it sends the data to a server where the data is stored and ready for investigation. This smart and low cost solution enables researchers, scientists and people who are interested, to track back former weather conditions. Which in turn functions as the
foundation of evidence-based decision making.
**Principle**

The ITT Smart Sense Weather Station continuously measures soil, air, precipitation and wind parameters of the study area. Via the heart of the station, the waspmode datalogger/ microcontroller, the data is processed and sent to a storage (sever) from which the data can be retrieved from anywhere of the world.
**Manual**

There are different working steps regarding the construction of the weather station. The sensors which are installed are: Soil humidity (Watermark [centibar]), Soil temperature (Pt1000 [°C]), Pluviometer (Generic [mm]), Air temperature, humidity, pressure (BME280[°C, kPa, % of relative humidity]), Wind velocity (WS3000 [m/s]), Wind direction (WS3000 [°relative to true north]), Pyranometer (SQ110). For constructing the smart sense waspmode box please check the detailed manuals which also include the codes for data processing and data transferring.
**Examples**

With the smart sense weather station, time series of weather data are generated and give insight about how it changes over some years.
