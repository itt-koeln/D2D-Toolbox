# Research for Impact online cource

*Posted 2021-02-03*

If you want the research that you are doing to have an impact, research that influences change in policy, practice and has a profound impact on society. Then have a look at this interesting - Free of charge - online course, which offered by the University of Cape Town, and Oxfam at this [link](https://www.coursera.org/learn/research-for-impact#syllabus).

It is also available along with other interesting online courses in our [ITT-Toolbox]({{ site.baseurl }}/). Have a look [here]({{ site.baseurl }}/toolbox/knowledge-transfer/).
