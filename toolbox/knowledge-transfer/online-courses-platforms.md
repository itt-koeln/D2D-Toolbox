**UN E-learning:**

https://unccelearn.org/

UN-E-learning offers free online climate change courses, backed by the UN system. The page provides a large number of freely accessible online courses in different thematic categories. Certificated courses are available.

---

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2018_10_classcentral-300x301.jpg)

**Class Central:**

https://www.class-central.com/

Class Central is a platform that centralizes online courses from a large number of Universities. Available courses cover a large variety of topics that reach from programming to languages and medical care. Most relevant in the context of the ITT are the science and programming sections which provide over a thousand online-courses from different universities.

---

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2018_10_theopenuni.jpg)

**The Open University:**

<http://www.openuniversity.edu/courses>

The Open University is a platform that provides bachelor and masters programs in different disciplines. Furthermore, the University offers a variety of certificated courses for different levels of education. The Open University is a pioneer in distance learning and the UK’s largest academic institution.

---

![]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/uploads/2018_10_open2study.jpg)

**Open2Study:**

https://www.open2study.com/

Open2Study is an Australian platform that provides free specialized short courses which are held entirely online, across the world and on a variety of subjects. Two categories of courses are available: free courses and accredited courses. Free courses have mostly a duration of one month and don’t require enrolment or payment while accredited courses allow users to obtain online bachelors or masters degrees.

---

**Hydrolearn:**

https://www.open2study.com/

Hydrolearn is a platform powered by OPENedX and provides free specialized short courses that are held entirely online,  on a variety of subjects related to Water and climate studies such as hydrology, hydraulics, Water-Energy-Food Nexus, and other.
