# Educational movies

*Posted 2018-09-27* · Category: Educational movies

The ITT focus on an innovative way to study the Natural Resources and environment through online learning and multi-media teaching tools. ITT staff together with partners from different regions throughout the years have dedicated time and resources to produce several educational movies in some case studies in those regions. Herein, this section of the Toolbox shows a selection of those movies:

1. ### **Competing uses of water in the Azrag Basin – Jordan**

The film explores the complexity of socio-economic systems, and scientifically assess drivers and pressures that led to the degradation of Azraq Oasis. It serves as a case study to explain the principles of IWRM and attempts to elaborate on different management responses. This movie includes interviews with: Prof.Dr.Lars Ribbe, Reda'a Al Youness, Dr.Marwan Al-Raggad, Nour Habjoka, Dr.Odeh Al-Jayyousi, Maysoon Al-Zo'bi. Written and narrated by Marc Haering, produced and edited by Simon Kuttnick, director of photography: Robert Wiezorek, executive production by ITT/Fachhochschule Köln and WEEC/The University of Jordan.

To watch this movie see the link below:
**<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LXlagegXJLA>**

2. ### **EBL-Nexus Project: Rainfed Agriculture challenges in the Ethiopian Highlands**

This documentary film is part of a team project on “Enquiry-Based Learning in the Curricula of Master-Level Courses in the Water and Land Nexus” (EBL-Nexus). It is a cooperation project between the German Institute for Technology and Resources Management in the Tropics and Subtropics (ITT), the University of Jordan, the University of Khartoum and Addis Ababa University and part of the DAAD programme ”Subject-related Partnerships with Institutions of Higher Education”.

It is a film by Daniel Metzke and Lorena Valeria Guzmán Wolfhard and narrated by Aline Bussmann, Daniel Metzke, Emmanuela Darious Lado, Prof. Dr. Lars Ribbe, Lorena Valeria Guzmán Wolfhard, Luis Antonio Esquivel Bustamante, Saw Min Aung.

To watch this movie see the link below:
**<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EYvHjOqlTj8>**

3. ### **Gezira Irrigation Scheme- A struggle for revival**

This documentary film is part of a team project on “Enquiry-Based Learning in the Curricula of Master-Level Courses in the Water and Land Nexus” (EBL-Nexus). It is a cooperation project between the German Institute for Technology and Resources Management in the Tropics and Subtropics (ITT), the University of Jordan, the University of Khartoum and Addis Ababa University and part of the DAAD programme ”Subject-related Partnerships with Institutions of Higher Education”. Gezira irrigation scheme is one of the largest irrigation projects in the world. Yet, farmers face difficulties to maintain their activities.

This film written and produced by Aline Bussmann, Directed and edited by Yidnekachew Shumete Desalegn and Aline Bussmann, and Produced by Kurat Pictures plc.

To watch this movie see the link below:
**<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hFxS1gGN4SU>**

4. ### **Tekezze Atbara, All that we share**

This documentary film is part of the communication activities of the ongoing research project “River Basin Simulation for improved water management in the Tekezze – Atbara Sub-basin, 2017-2018”. The research project is implemented by the Hydraulics Research Center (lead), the Ethiopian Institute for Water Resources – Addis Ababa University, and IHE–Delft. The DUPC2 program of IHE Delft funds the project. The objective of the documentary film is to spotlight the Tekezze – Atbara River (a sub-basin of the Nile), by giving information about the river itself, land, water, and people living in the basin. HRC-Sudan and Cewas produced the film, with financial support from ITT – TH Köln (Germany), and DUPC2 program of IHE-Delft.

To watch this movie see the link below:
**<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bHETSD4mdLk>**

5. ### **CapWater - Developing capacities in the field of monitoring and assessment of water resources**

This documentary film is introductory about CapWater project and its three main topics: monitoring, data management, and modeling and their relevance for sustainable water resources management. It was produced with the support of German Academic Exchange Service (DAAD), Institute for Technology and Resources Management in the Tropics and Subtropics (ITT), Independent University- Bangladesh, Universidade Federal Fluminense- Brazil, and Vietnam Academy for Water Resources (VAWR).
